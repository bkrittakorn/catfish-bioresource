About the Website

Knowledge for Catfish Bioresources

Catfish of the genus Clarias represent a vital freshwater bioresource in Thailand, playing key roles in biodiversity, food security, aquaculture economics, and the utilization of genetic resources. Particularly significant are the broadhead catfish (Clarias macrocephalus), a species native to Thailand, and the African sharptooth catfish (Clarias gariepinus), an introduced species widely farmed commercially due to its high growth rate and environmental adaptability. Furthermore, hybrid catfish are extensively produced to enhance efficiency in aquaculture systems. However, environmental changes, habitat loss, the introduction of non-native species, and the risk of genetic introgression between native and non-native catfish may impact genetic diversity and the long-term integrity of biological resources. Simultaneously, genomic data and genetic resource documentation for catfish in Thailand require further research and development to support conservation and sustainable utilization. The CATFISH BIORESOURCE Project focuses on studying, compiling, and advancing knowledge on the biodiversity and genetics of catfish. By integrating genomics, population genetics, comparative genomics, and bioinformatics, the project investigates genetic diversity, population structure, evolutionary relationships, genetic traits related to growth and sex determination, as well as the development of molecular tools for species identification and genetic contamination screening. Additionally, the project emphasizes the development of a catfish bioresource and genomic database to serve as a scientific repository and dissemination platform for researchers, students, aquaculture operators, and relevant agencies. The data can be applied toward native catfish conservation, monitoring the impacts of non-native species, selective breeding, and enhancing sustainable production efficiency. These efforts establish a crucial foundation connecting biodiversity conservation with sustainable genetic resource utilization, supporting the bioeconomy and the long-term security of aquatic resources in Thailand.
